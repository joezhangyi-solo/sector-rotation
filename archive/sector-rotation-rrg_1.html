Tail — how far back each sector's path is drawn. At 1 you see only today's position; at 8 weeks you see the route it took to get there, which is what tells you whether a sector is arriving or leaving.

Date — rewinds every sector to where it stood on an earlier date, tails redrawn for the periods before it. Animate steps this forward one period at a time.

Relative strength is measured against SPY on a total-return basis. The horizontal axis is smoothed relative strength, the vertical axis is the momentum of that relative strength; both are normalised so 100 is the benchmark. Sectors generally travel clockwise: improving, then leading, then weakening, then lagging. This is a working approximation of the JdK RS-Ratio and RS-Momentum method, not the licensed StockCharts calculation, so exact coordinates will differ from theirs.
